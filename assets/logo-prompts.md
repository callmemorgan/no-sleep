# NoSleep - Logo & Asset Image Generation Prompts

This document contains 6 self-contained image-generation prompts (optimized for DALL-E 3, Midjourney, SDXL, etc.) for logo and asset variations of the coffee cup motif for **NoSleep**, a macOS menu bar app that prevents a Mac from sleeping.

---

## 1. macOS App Icon (Full Color HIG Style)

- **Title:** macOS App Icon (Apple HIG Squircle)
- **Prompt:** A 3D rendered macOS application icon for a utility named NoSleep, featuring a modern ceramic coffee cup and saucer motif representing alertness. Designed strictly following Apple Human Interface Guidelines (HIG): rounded-square squircle tile canvas, realistic ceramic material texture, subtle depth and layered shadows, soft top-center lighting with ambient drop shadows. A sleek white ceramic coffee cup with warm wispy steam rising, set against a smooth gradient background transitioning from rich warm indigo to deep slate grey. Clean centered composition, full color, 1024x1024 resolution, premium macOS system utility icon, isolated on a white background, no text, --ar 1:1
- **What to tweak:** Adjust the background squircle gradient colors (e.g. deep royal blue to indigo vs warm orange-amber) to change the overall warmth and brand mood.

## 2. Menu Bar Template Icon (Pure Black Silhouette)

- **Title:** Menu Bar Template Icon (18pt Silhouette)
- **Prompt:** A minimalist flat vector glyph of a coffee cup and saucer icon, designed specifically as a macOS menu bar template icon. Pure solid black silhouette isolated on a clean transparent background. Highly simplified vector geometry optimized for micro-scale clarity and legibility at 18pt size. Bold clean outlines, zero gradients, zero textures, zero drop shadows. Pure black graphic glyph, centered composition, ultra-clean UI icon graphic, --ar 1:1
- **What to tweak:** Render both a solid filled cup variant (representing the app "Active / Prevent Sleep ON" state) and an outline stroke variant (representing "Inactive / OFF" state).

## 3. Flat Minimal Line-Art Variant

- **Title:** Flat Minimal Line-Art Variant
- **Prompt:** A modern minimal monoline vector logo of a coffee cup and saucer for NoSleep app branding. Elegant 2D line art with consistent medium line weight, soft rounded corners, and balanced geometric proportions. Simple continuous outline of a coffee cup with minimal vertical steam lines. Crisp contrast using dark charcoal grey lines on a neutral light grey background. 100% flat design, no gradients, no shadows, centered composition, ultra-clean branding graphic, --ar 1:1
- **What to tweak:** Adjust the line weight (thin 2pt vs bold 4pt) or invert colors (white lines on dark charcoal background) for dark UI presentations.

## 4. Playful / Illustrated Variant

- **Title:** Playful Illustrated Steam & Sleep Motif
- **Prompt:** A charming and whimsical vector illustration of a steaming coffee cup logo for NoSleep. Modern flat illustration style with bold outlines and vibrant warm accent colors. The rising steam from the hot coffee cup cleverly forms a stylized sleeping crescent moon with a diagonal slash through it (or crossed-out "ZZ" symbols), creatively representing staying awake. Warm inviting color scheme featuring coffee brown, glowing amber orange, and soft cream accents on a solid deep navy blue background. Friendly centered composition, high-resolution vector artwork, no text, --ar 1:1
- **What to tweak:** Experiment with the steam shape—forming either a crossed-out moon, a crossed-out "ZZZ", or energetic lightning sparks above the coffee cup.

---

## 5. Monochrome Variant for Light/Dark Menu Bars

- **Title:** Universal Monochrome Adaptive Logo
- **Prompt:** A high-contrast monochrome vector emblem featuring a stylized coffee cup and saucer icon. Modern graphic iconography designed to adapt seamlessly across both light and dark backgrounds. Pure solid black and white shapes using clever negative space cuts to define the cup handle, cup body, and steam wisps. Flat 2D vector style, no greyscale shades, no gradients, clean geometric vector art, centered composition, isolated on a neutral background, --ar 1:1
- **What to tweak:** Invert the positive and negative space (black-on-white vs white-on-black) to test legibility on dark and light menu bar states.

---

## 6. Modern Gradient / Glassy macOS Variant

- **Title:** Modern Glassmorphism & Gradient Icon
- **Prompt:** A modern macOS-style app icon featuring a glassmorphic coffee cup made of translucent frosted glass with glowing liquid inside. Vibrant gradient canvas background transitioning smoothly from electric neon orange to deep purple-magenta. Translucent glass materials with realistic specular reflections, soft frosted refraction, and subtle ambient light glow around the cup handle and rim. Apple macOS Sonoma/Sequoia design aesthetic, 3D icon rendering, sleek rounded squircle canvas frame, rich tactile depth, soft drop shadows, --ar 1:1
- **What to tweak:** Adjust the glass frostiness and transparency level or alter the background gradient palette (e.g. vibrant sunrise orange vs sleek ocean blue) to fine-tune visual intensity.
